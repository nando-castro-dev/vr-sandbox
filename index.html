🎮 Controls

🖥️ Computer

  • W A S D move · Space/C up/down
  • Right-drag look around · L or double-click sky = mouse-look (Esc exits)
  • Click an object to select · drag it to move
  • Wheel on selected = raise/lower
  • Q/E rotate · R/F bigger/smaller
  • 1–6 spawn · T next color
  • Ctrl+D duplicate · Del delete · Ctrl+Z undo · G snap

🥽 VR headset (Quest)

  • Enter VR = neon night world · Enter AR = passthrough: your build sits in your real room
  • Left wrist = menu. Point right hand, pull trigger to tap
  • Grip or trigger on an object = grab it, move your hand, let go
  • While holding: right stick ←→ rotate, ↑↓ resize
  • VR: point at floor + trigger = teleport
  • Right stick ←→ (not holding) = snap turn
  • AR: point at your floor or a table, then spawn from the wrist to place it there. Wrist 👓 Passthrough flips between your room and the night world

📱 Phone

  • Tap to select · drag object to move · drag sky to look · two-finger drag to walk
